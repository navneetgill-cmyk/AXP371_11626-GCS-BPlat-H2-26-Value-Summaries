5X Membership Rewards Points on Flights and Prepaid Lodging Booked Through AmexTravel.com or the Amex Travel App™

Basic Card Members will get 1 Membership Rewards® point for each dollar charged for eligible purchases on their Business Platinum Card® or on an Employee Card on their Card Account.

Basic Card Members will earn 4 additional points (for a total of 5 Membership Rewards points or 5x points) for each dollar charged for eligible air and lodging bookings on any Card on the Account, minus cancellations and credits. Eligible air and lodging bookings are limited to bookings of: (i) air tickets on scheduled flights booked through AmexTravel.com or the Amex Travel App™; (ii) prepaid hotel reservations booked through American Express Travel (through AmexTravel.com, the Amex Travel App™, or by calling the phone number on the back of the eligible Card); and (iii) prepaid short-term rental reservations booked with Airbnb through AmexTravel.com or the Amex Travel App™. To complete a short-term rental reservation with Airbnb through AmexTravel.com or the Amex Travel App™, the eligible Card Member must have an Airbnb account linked to their American Express online account. Eligible air and lodging bookings do not include: charter flights, private jet flights, flights that are part of tours, cruises, or travel packages, scheduled flights booked over the phone, ticketing or similar service fees, ticket cancellation or change fees, property fees or similar fees, hotel group reservations or events, insurance fees, interest charges, or purchases of cash equivalents. Eligible prepaid hotel bookings that are modified directly with the hotel will not be eligible for additional points. Additional fees and purchases associated with your prepaid short-term rental booking will not be eligible for additional points (e.g., pool heating fees or pet fees).

Bonuses that may be received with your Card on other purchase categories or in connection with promotions or offers from American Express cannot be combined with this benefit. Purchases eligible for multiple additional point bonuses will only receive the highest individual eligible bonus. Any portion of a charge that the Basic Card Member elects to cover through redemption of Membership Rewards points is not eligible to receive points. Additional terms and restrictions apply. Please allow up to 12 weeks after the qualifying purchase is charged to your Card Account for Membership Rewards points to be posted to the account.

Merchants are assigned codes based on what they primarily sell. We group certain merchant codes into categories that are eligible for additional points. A purchase with a merchant will not earn additional points if the merchant’s code is not included in an additional points category. You may not receive additional points if we receive inaccurate information or are otherwise unable to identify your purchase as eligible for an additional points category. For example, you may not receive additional points when: a merchant uses a third-party to sell their products or services, a merchant uses a third-party to process or submit your transaction to us (e.g., using mobile or wireless card readers), or you choose to make a purchase using a third-party payment account or make a purchase using a mobile or digital wallet.

To be eligible for this benefit, Card Account(s) must not be cancelled or past due at the time of additional point fulfillment. $600 Lodging Credit

Effective Ja‌nuary 1, 2027, Fine Hotels + Resorts® bookings will require a minimum two-night stay to be eligible for the $600 Lodging Credit.

Basic Card Members on U.S. Business Platinum Card Accounts (“Card Account”) are eligible to receive up to $300 in statement credits semi-annually (January to June, and July to December) for up to a total of $600 in statement credits per calendar year, when they or Employee Business Platinum Card Members on the Card Account use their Cards to pay for new prepaid Fine Hotels + Resorts® and The Hotel Collection bookings made through AmexTravel.com, the Amex Travel App™, or by calling the phone number on the back of the eligible Card and new prepaid Airbnb Luxe bookings with Airbnb made through AmexTravel.com or the Amex Travel App™; or when Employee Business Expense Card Members on the Card Account pay for new prepaid bookings for The Hotel Collection made through AmexTravel.com, the Amex Travel App™, or by calling the phone number of the back of the eligible Card, and new prepaid Airbnb Luxe bookings with Airbnb made through AmexTravel.com or the Amex Travel App™. Bookings of The Hotel Collection and Airbnb Luxe properties require a minimum stay of two consecutive nights to receive statement credits. To complete an Airbnb Luxe booking with Airbnb made through AmexTravel.com or the Amex Travel App™, the eligible Card Member must have an Airbnb account linked to their American Express online account. Prepaid bookings are referred to as “Pay Now” on AmexTravel.com and the Amex Travel App™. Purchases by both the Basic Card Member and any Employee Card Members on the Card Account are eligible for statement credits. However, the total amount of statement credits for eligible purchases will not exceed $600 per calendar year, per Card Account. Eligible bookings do not include interest charges, cancellation fees, property fees or other similar fees, or any charges by a property to you (whether for your booking, your stay or otherwise).

Statement credits are typically received within a few days, however it may take 90 days after an eligible prepaid hotel or Airbnb Luxe booking is charged to the Card Account. Eligible bookings must be processed before June 30th, 11:59PM Central Time, to be eligible for statement credits within the January to June benefit period, and December 31st, 11:59PM Central Time, to be eligible for statement credits within the July to December benefit period. American Express relies on the merchant’s processing of transactions to determine the transaction date. If there is a delay in the merchant submitting the transaction to us or if the merchant uses another date as the transaction date, then your purchase may not earn the statement credit benefit for the benefit period in which you made the purchase. For example, if an eligible purchase is made on December 31st but the merchant processes the transaction such that it is identified to us as occurring on January 1st, then the statement credit available in the next eligibility calendar year benefit period will be applied, if available. If you have transferred to a different Card at the time the merchant submits the transaction, you may not receive the statement credit. Statement credit(s) may not be received or may be reversed if the booking is refunded, cancelled, or modified. If the Card Account is cancelled or past due, it may not qualify to receive a statement credit. If American Express does not receive information that identifies your transaction as eligible, you will not receive the statement credits. For example, your transaction will not be eligible if it is a booking: (i) made with a property not included in the Fine Hotels + Resorts or The Hotel Collection programs, (ii) made with an Airbnb property not included in the Airbnb Luxe program, (iii) made outside of American Express Travel, or (iv) made with an ineligible Card. If American Express, in its sole discretion, determines that you have engaged in or intend to engage in any manner of abuse, misuse, or gaming in connection with this benefit, American Express will not have an obligation to provide, and may reverse, any statement credits provided to you. Instead, the statement credit may be applied to your Pay In Full balance. For additional information, call the number on the back of your Card. Please refer to AmericanExpress.com/FHR and AmericanExpress.com/HC for more information about Fine Hotels + Resorts and The Hotel Collection, respectively.


$250 Experiences and Services Credit

Basic Card Members on U.S. Consumer Platinum Card® or U.S. Business Platinum Card® Accounts who make a prepaid Airbnb Luxe booking with Airbnb through AmexTravel.com or the Amex Travel App™ can earn up to $250 in statement credits after they or any Additional Card Members on the Card Account use their Card to make eligible Airbnb Experiences purchases. However, the total amount of statement credits for eligible Airbnb Experiences purchases will not exceed $250 per calendar year per Card Account.

The Basic Card Member or any Additional Card Member on the Card Account must first use their eligible Card to make one prepaid Airbnb Luxe booking with Airbnb through AmexTravel.com or the Amex Travel App™. Once their Airbnb Luxe booking is confirmed, they will unlock the $250 Airbnb Experiences Credit starting from the day their booking request was submitted through the end of that calendar year. If the eligible Card Member submits a booking request that is confirmed in the following calendar year, the $250 Airbnb Experiences Credit will be unlocked through the end of that year. To complete an Airbnb Luxe booking with Airbnb through AmexTravel.com or the Amex Travel App™, the eligible Card Member must have an Airbnb account linked to their American Express online account. After making an Airbnb Luxe booking, Basic Card Members can confirm that the $250 Airbnb Experiences Credit benefit has been unlocked by logging into their American Express online account and checking the Benefits section, receiving a confirmation email from American Express sent to the email associated with their eligible Card Account, or calling the number on the back of their Card. After an Airbnb Luxe booking has been requested, the Airbnb host has 24 hours to accept or decline the request. If the host declines the request or doesn’t respond within 24 hours, the booking status will be updated as “Declined” or “Expired,” and the $250 Airbnb Experiences Credit benefit will remain locked. If the booking is confirmed, please call the number on the back of the Card if the benefit has not been unlocked 72 hours after the Airbnb Luxe booking confirmation.

After the benefit is unlocked on the Card Account, to receive the statement credits, by De‌cember 3‌1st of the same calendar year as the Airbnb Luxe booking confirmation, the Basic Card Member or any Additional Card Member on the Card Account must make eligible Airbnb Experience purchases, which include only Airbnb Experiences sold directly by Airbnb at www.airbnb.com/experiences or through the Experiences tab within the Airbnb app. Other purchases from Airbnb are not eligible. Certain Experience bookings direct you from Airbnb to a third-party partner platform to make the purchase (for example, live event tickets) and are not eligible.

At least one qualifying Airbnb Luxe booking must be made with an eligible Card and confirmed by Airbnb by De‌cember 3‌1st and the booking must not be cancelled for the benefit to remain unlocked on that Card Account for that calendar year. Statement credits may not be received or may be reversed if the qualifying Airbnb Luxe booking is cancelled at any time for any reason. If multiple qualifying Airbnb Luxe bookings are confirmed in the same calendar year, at least one qualifying Airbnb Luxe booking must not be cancelled to keep the benefit unlocked and the Card Account eligible to earn up to $250 in statement credits for eligible Airbnb Experiences purchases made through De‌cember 3‌1st of the same calendar year as the Airbnb Luxe booking confirmation.

If all qualifying Airbnb Luxe bookings are cancelled for any reason, the benefit will no longer be considered unlocked and any statement credits previously received may be reversed. To unlock the benefit again in the same calendar year, the eligible Card Member must make another prepaid Airbnb Luxe booking with Airbnb through AmexTravel.com or the Amex Travel App™ which is confirmed by Airbnb by December 31st of that year. Any Airbnb Experiences purchases made prior to the benefit regaining unlocked status will not be eligible for statement credits. Once an eligible Card Account is confirmed to have unlocked the benefit again, a new, eligible Airbnb Experience must be purchased and charged to the same Card Account to receive any statement credits in that calendar year, up to $250 per calendar year per Card Account. If an eligible Card Member transfers to a different Card, they will lose access to the benefit that was unlocked on their former Card.

Please allow up to 8 weeks after an eligible purchase is charged to the Card Account for statement credit(s) to be posted to the Account. Please call the number on the back of the Card if statement credits have not posted after 8 weeks from the date an eligible purchase is charged to the Card Account. American Express relies on the merchant’s processing of transactions to determine the transaction date. If there is a delay in the merchant submitting the transaction to us or if the merchant uses another date as the transaction date, then the purchase may not earn the statement credit benefit for the benefit period in which the Card Member made the purchase. For example, if an Airbnb Experiences purchase is made on De‌cember 3‌1st, but the merchant processes the transaction such that it is identified to us as occurring on January 1st, the purchase will not be eligible for the prior year’s statement credit. If the Card Member has transferred to a different Card at the time the merchant submits the transaction, they may not receive the statement credit. Statement credit(s) may not be received or may be reversed if all qualifying Airbnb Luxe bookings or eligible Airbnb Experiences purchases are refunded, cancelled, or modified for any reason.

American Express also relies on information provided to us by the merchant to identify eligible purchases. If American Express does not receive information that identifies the transaction as eligible for this benefit, the Basic Card Member will not receive the statement credit. Basic Card Members may not receive the statement credit if we receive inaccurate information or are otherwise unable to identify the purchase as eligible, if a transaction is made with an electronic wallet or through a third party (such as an app store), or if the merchant uses a mobile or wireless card reader to process it. For example, an Airbnb Experiences purchase will not be eligible if (i) the qualifying Airbnb Luxe booking is never confirmed (ii) it is made in a different calendar year from the confirmation of the qualifying Airbnb Luxe booking, (iii) it is made after the qualifying Airbnb Luxe booking has been cancelled (if no other qualifying Airbnb Luxe booking is maintained), (iv) it is purchased anywhere other than directly through Airbnb.com or the Airbnb app, or (v) it is charged to an ineligible Card Account.
